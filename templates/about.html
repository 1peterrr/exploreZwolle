About Explore Zwolle

Explore Zwolle is a web application designed to help students discover useful and interesting places in zwolle while studying there .

Users can share recommendations for restaurants, attractions, cafes, nightlife and other places they have visited. Each recommendation can include a location, category, rating, description, image and the name of the person making the recommendation.

What can you do?

  • View recommendations from other students
  • Add new places and recommendations
  • Edit existing recommendations
  • Search for places
  • Filter recommendations by category
  • Give places a rating from 1 to 5

Purpose

The aim of the application is to provide a simple way for students to share their experiences and help other students find places worth visiting during their time in zwolle.
